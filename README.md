# **Modelling Inflation with Random Forests**

This repository contains the data, code and output of the study describe in the paper **"Modelling Inflation with Random Forests"**.

## **Abstract**

* **Aim:** Investigating the utility of machine learning methods, specifically Random Forests, in inflation modelling, with particular emphasis on the exchange rate and the level of foreign exchange reserves.  
* **Methodology:** Utilizing publicly available data regarding the inflation rate and other macroeconomic factors in Poland over a period of 20 years, Random Forests were applied to construct three models predicting future price growth based on selected explanatory variables.  
* **Results:** Random Forests applied to inflation modelling in their basic form, without advanced feature engineering techniques and with limited data availability, do not outperform classical time series modelling methods such as Linear Regression, ARMA, or ARIMA, but merely match their performance.  
* **Implications and recommendations:** The obtained results demonstrate the limitations of machine learning methods under conditions of limited data access and computational power, whilst highlighting their potential for further improvement in a data-rich environment.  
* **Originality/value:** The application of Random Forests in modelling Polish inflation data, combined with an investigation into the potential benefits of including the exchange rate and the level of foreign exchange reserves as explanatory variables.

**Keywords:** inflation, econometric modelling, machine learning, random forests
