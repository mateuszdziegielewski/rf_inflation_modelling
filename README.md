# **Modelling Inflation with Random Forests**

This repository contains the data, code and output of the study describe in the paper **"Modelling Inflation with Random Forests"**.

## **Abstract**

* **Aim:** Investigating the utility of machine learning methods, specifically Random Forests, in inflation modelling, with particular emphasis on the exchange rate and the level of foreign exchange reserves.

* **Methodology:** Utilizing publicly available data regarding the inflation rate and other macroeconomic factors in Poland over a period of 20 years, Random Forests were applied to construct three models predicting future price growth based on selected explanatory variables.

* **Results:** The empirical findings demonstrate that the Random Forest model outperformed the traditional ARIMAX and Linear Regression models in overall predictive accuracy. However, the inclusion of the USD/PLN exchange rate and foreign exchange reserves did not improve the results, while even deteriorating Random Forest performance. Notably, model performance varied strictly by macroeconomic regime.

* **Implications and recommendations:** The findings highlight that non-linear machine learning methods are vastly superior during structural shocks and stabilization periods. However, traditional models remain crucial as safe anchors during unexpected administrative interventions, strongly advocating for Ensemble Forecasting in modern central banking.

* **Originality/value:** A novel aspect of this paper is the application of Random Forests in modelling Polish inflation data, combined with an investigation into the potential benefits of including the exchange rate and the level of foreign exchange reserves as explanatory variables.

**Keywords:** inflation, econometric modelling, machine learning, random forests
